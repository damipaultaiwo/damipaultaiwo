## Projects

**[SQL Ecommerce Analytics](https://github.com/damipaultaiwo/SQL-Ecommerce-Analytics)**
Nine queries against the Olist Brazilian ecommerce dataset, roughly 100,000 orders across eight relational tables, moving from joins and aggregation to CTEs and window functions. The headline finding is that delivery performance drives satisfaction more than anything else: on time orders average 4.29 stars against 1.73 for orders more than a week late.

**[Machine Learning and NLP Coursework](https://github.com/damipaultaiwo/MBAI5310G-AI-Programming-Dami-Paul-Taiwo)**
K-Means customer segmentation, decision tree risk prediction, a model evaluation and fairness audit using SHAP and LIME, and an NLP text classification and routing pipeline.

**[Canada Housing 2026: A Generation Locked Out](https://damipaultaiwo.github.io/Portfolio/#projects)**
An infographic built on Statistics Canada and CMHC data showing a Canadian home now costs close to eight times median household income, with Gen Z homeownership at 21 percent.

**[Redesigning Nightingale's Coxcomb Diagram](https://damipaultaiwo.github.io/Portfolio/#projects)**
Six iterations of a redesign of Florence Nightingale's mortality chart. A study in choosing the encoding that answers the question rather than the one that looks impressive.

**[The Broken Contract: Housing and Wages in Toronto](https://damipaultaiwo.github.io/Portfolio/#projects)**
A group explainer video tracing a 31 percentage point affordability gap between 1982 and 2025 through two contrasting personas.
